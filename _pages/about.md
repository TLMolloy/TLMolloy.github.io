I am a Senior Lecturer in the Department of Electrical and Computer Systems Engineering and Monash Robotics at Monash University, Australia.
I am interested in the foundations of robust, reactive, and interpretable intelligent robots and autonomous systems.
My research combines decision, control, and information theory with artificial intelligence and machine learning to understand how machines and other agents can (and should) learn objectives, decide what to observe, detect changes, and act in uncertain environments. 
My recent work has focused on [inverse optimal control](/research/#inverse-optimal-control), [partially observed Markov decision processes (POMDPs)](/research/#pomdps), [quickest change detection](/research/#change-detection), [dynamic and differential game theory](/research/#game-theory), and [event-based sensing and perception](/research/#event-cameras).

Prior to joining Monash, I was a Senior Lecturer in Mechatronics in the School of Engineering at the Australian National University (ANU); a Research Fellow at the University of Melbourne; and an Advance Queensland Research Fellow at the Queensland University of Technology (QUT), where I was embedded with Boeing Research & Technology Australia.
I received the B.E. degree in Aerospace Avionics and the Ph.D. degree in Robotics and Autonomous Systems from QUT.
