I am a Senior Lecturer in the Department of Electrical and Computer Systems Engineering and Monash Robotics at Monash University, Australia.

My research focuses on **learning, decision-making, and control under uncertainty for robots and autonomous systems**:

- [**Learn**](/research/#learning) what people and other agents are trying to achieve by observing how they behave, so that robots can acquire skills from demonstrations and anticipate the intent of others (inverse optimal control, inverse reinforcement learning, learning from demonstration, online optimal control).
- [**Decide**](/research/#decision-making) what to sense and when to act when information is incomplete, noisy, or changing without warning (partially observed Markov decision processes, active sensing, quickest change detection, event-based perception).
- [**Control**](/research/#control) the motion and dynamics of robots and autonomous systems while also interacting with other agents, including people and vehicles, so that each can pursue its own goals safely (optimal control, dynamic and differential game theory, vision-based aircraft collision avoidance, pursuit–evasion).

Read more on the [research](/research/) page.

Prior to joining Monash, I was a Senior Lecturer in Mechatronics in the School of Engineering at the Australian National University (ANU); a Research Fellow at the University of Melbourne; and an Advance Queensland Research Fellow at the Queensland University of Technology (QUT), where I was embedded with Boeing Research & Technology Australia. I received the B.E. degree in Aerospace Avionics and the Ph.D. degree in Robotics and Autonomous Systems from QUT.
